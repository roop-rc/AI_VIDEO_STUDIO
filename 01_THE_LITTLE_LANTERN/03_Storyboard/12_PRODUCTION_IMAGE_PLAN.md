## Planning decision

Plan **28 genuinely new production compositions**, each with one stable PI ID and one matching primary shot. The other **27 shots** use existing masters, controlled derivatives, composites, editing/effects or a title graphic. This is a planning estimate, not a generation quota or approval. It is more conservative than the earlier approximate 20–24 dedicated-composition estimate because different camera directions, hand actions, wet faces and location-specific light inserts cannot safely be collapsed. It remains substantially fewer than 55 independent images.

PI IDs identify planned compositions, not existing files or new canonical references. No future output path/extension is promised before tool selection. A reference-controlled new composition still counts as NEW_PRODUCTION_IMAGE; a derivative is not a free independent generation. Budget approval is required for any generative local edit as well as initial images.

MASTER means an existing canonical visual foundation, with permitted framing/minor local editorial finishing. DERIVE_FROM_PRODUCTION_IMAGE means a constrained crop/local transformation of its named PI, never a new independent composition. ROUGH_COMPOSITE describes the assembly method; final production quality still requires credible scale, perspective, masks, lighting and contact. It does not authorise final use of animatic insets. EDIT_EFFECT primarily uses editorial masks, local lighting, weather or transitions. TITLE_GRAPHIC uses approved wording in the editor.

Proposed blocking uses right-hand normal Lumi carry, except the approved two-hand shelter, outward screen-right and homeward screen-left where a shot exposes direction. This is consistent staging proposed for review, not a change to story or a claim of a pre-existing screen-axis lock. Preserve local eye lines; do not flip canonical images to solve identity or prop problems.
