# 1. Character Consistency Principle

If a visual characteristic matters later, define it before image
generation.

For *The Little Lantern*, **Lumi and Maya require strict visual
consistency**. The Shopkeeper and Old Woman require moderate
consistency. Background villagers follow population-level design rules.

The Character Bible is the source of truth for all later character
prompts, reference images, storyboards, production images and animation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. Lumi

## Identity

**Name:** Lumi\
**Role:** Main protagonist\
**Type:** Small handheld lantern\
**Personality:** Gentle, observant, initially insecure, courageous when
needed\
**Character Arc:** "My light is too small to matter" → "My small light
can still be useful."

## Physical Design

Lumi does **not** have cartoon eyes or a mouth. Emotion is communicated
through flame behaviour, movement, lighting, framing and sound.

-   Approximately 18 cm tall
-   Old handcrafted brass construction
-   Warm aged brass rather than shiny gold
-   Rectangular body with gently rounded corners
-   Four glass panels
-   Small circular ventilation holes near the top
-   Curved brass carrying handle
-   Slightly crooked right side
-   Small dent near the lower-left corner
-   Several fine scratches from years of use
-   Slightly cloudy glass
-   Tiny warm amber flame

Her physical construction must remain recognisably identical throughout
the film.

## Signature Features

1.  Subtly crooked right side
2.  Small lower-left dent
3.  Curved carrying handle
4.  Tiny amber flame
5.  Weathered aged-brass finish

## Emotional Language

  Emotion            Visual Behaviour
  ------------------ --------------------------------------------
  Happy              Flame becomes slightly warmer and steadier
  Sad                Flame lowers subtly
  Nervous            Small irregular flicker
  Afraid             Flame bends/shakes in wind
  Determined         Flame stabilises
  Exhausted          Very small but persistent flame
  Proud / peaceful   Calm, steady amber glow

### Critical Rule

Lumi's flame may change subtly for emotional storytelling but must never
become an enormous magical flame.

Her character arc does not involve gaining greater physical power.

# 3. Maya

## Identity

**Name:** Maya\
**Age:** Approximately 10\
**Role:** Lumi's companion\
**Personality:** Curious, kind, adventurous, observant\
**Emotional Arc:** Confident explorer → frightened and lost → trusting →
relieved

## Appearance

-   10-year-old girl
-   Warm medium-brown skin
-   Large expressive dark-brown eyes
-   Dark-brown wavy hair
-   Shoulder-length hair
-   Hair parted slightly to the left
-   Small loose strand frequently falling near her forehead
-   Soft rounded facial features
-   Natural child proportions
-   No makeup
-   No adult styling

## Signature Clothing

Maya wears one core outfit throughout the adventure.

-   **Outer clothing:** Mustard-yellow hooded raincoat
-   **Underlayer:** Muted cream knitted sweater
-   **Bottom:** Dark teal trousers
-   **Shoes:** Brown ankle boots
-   **Accessory:** Small forest-green canvas satchel

### Maya Colour Identity

The mustard-yellow raincoat provides strong visual separation from the

# 5. Maya + Lumi Scale Relationship

Approximate scale:

``` text
Maya ≈ 140 cm tall
Lumi ≈ 18 cm tall
```

Lumi must comfortably fit in Maya's hand.

During normal movement, Maya carries Lumi using the top brass handle.

During the storm, Maya may use both hands to shield Lumi.

Lumi must never become:

-   Handbag-sized
-   Torso-sized
-   Floating
-   Magically attached to Maya
-   Electrically illuminated
-   A bare candle without a lantern enclosure

# 13. Workflow Improvement 002

## Character Specification vs Character Lock

Character development is now formally divided into two checkpoints:

``` text
Written Character Specification
        ↓
Specification Approval
        ↓
Visual Style Bible
        ↓
Master Reference Generation
        ↓
Reference Review
        ↓
Visual Character Lock
```

## Reason

A written character description can be internally consistent while still
producing an unattractive, impractical or difficult-to-reproduce visual
design.

Therefore, the studio will not consider a character fully locked until
approved master reference images exist.
