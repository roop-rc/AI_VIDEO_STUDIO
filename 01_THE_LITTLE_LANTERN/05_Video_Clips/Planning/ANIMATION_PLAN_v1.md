## 2. Animation implementation classes

Exactly one primary class per shot:

- **A — EDITORIAL STILL MOTION:** pan, push/pull, reframe, dissolve, fade or intentional static hold. No AI video.
- **B — EDITORIAL EFFECT / ENVIRONMENT MOTION:** local flames, light, rain, mist, clouds, exposure or reflection work. No AI video.
- **C — SUBTLE AI IMAGE-TO-VIDEO:** restrained living motion, here optional and never permission to regenerate an image.
- **D — CHARACTER ACTION AI VIDEO:** meaningful single-character physical action, short and bounded.
- **E — COMPLEX / HIGH-RISK AI VIDEO:** required lift or run where hand/lantern mechanics and identity warrant particular scrutiny; not more attempts.
- **F — HYBRID:** optional short living-motion section with rain/flame work performed editorially on stable source/endpoints, avoiding a longer weather-plus-character generation. Only 09C/12D use it, and only if approved; otherwise their editorial fallback applies.

AI_REQUIRED is the recommended minimum if the stated action is to be shown in motion, not a generation authorization or claim no human animator could achieve it. C/F optional rows retain their single proposed class; their fallback is a contingency, not a second assigned class. Optional clips are excluded from minimum clip/second totals. Secondary cosmetic flicker does not reclassify an A shot as B. No tool-specific video prompts are prepared.
