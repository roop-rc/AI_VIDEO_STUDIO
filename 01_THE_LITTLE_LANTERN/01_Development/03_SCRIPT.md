# The Little Lantern --- Production Script v1.0

## 00:00--00:12 --- Opening / Bellwood

**VISUAL:** Fade from black. A wide aerial view reveals snowy mountains
at sunset. Nestled in the valley is Bellwood. One by one, warm lanterns
begin glowing throughout the village.

**NARRATOR:**\
"High among the mountains stood a little village called Bellwood. Every
evening, as the sun disappeared behind the snowy peaks, hundreds of
lanterns came to life."

**AUDIO:** Gentle orchestral opening. Soft mountain wind. Very faint
village ambience.

**EDITING:** Slow cinematic push toward Bellwood.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 00:12--00:25 --- The Great Lantern

**VISUAL:** Camera travels upward from the village toward an old stone
tower. The enormous Great Lantern ignites, throwing brilliant golden
light across the valley.

**NARRATOR:**\
"And high above them all burned the Great Lantern. Its enormous flame
could be seen from far across the valley."

**AUDIO:** Music gently swells. Deep, warm ignition sound.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 00:25--00:43 --- Meet Lumi

**VISUAL:** Cut from the enormous flame to a tiny flickering flame. Pull
back to reveal Lumi sitting on a wooden shelf inside a cosy lantern
workshop.

Lumi is small, scratched and slightly crooked.

**NARRATOR:**\
"But at the edge of the village lived a very different lantern. Her name
was Lumi."

"Lumi was small enough to fit into a child's hands, and her flame was no
bigger than a firefly."

**AUDIO:** Intimate music. Workshop ambience. Soft fire crackle.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 00:43--01:00 --- Lumi's Insecurity

**VISUAL:** Through the workshop window, Lumi watches beautiful lanterns
glowing outside. Show impressive lanterns in the square and, finally,
the Great Lantern dominating the skyline.

Return to Lumi and her tiny flame.

**NARRATOR:**\
"Every night, Lumi watched the other lanterns shine."

**LUMI --- INTERNAL VOICE, quiet:**\
"What good is a light this small?"

**VISUAL:** Her flame dips slightly.

**AUDIO:** Music becomes tender and slightly melancholy.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 01:00--01:20 --- Maya Chooses Lumi

**VISUAL:** Daytime. The workshop door opens. Maya enters and explores
shelves filled with lanterns.

She notices Lumi.

Maya gently lifts her.

**MAYA:**\
"This one."

**SHOPKEEPER:**\
"There are brighter lanterns."

**MAYA, smiling:**\
"I like this one."

**VISUAL:** Lumi's flame becomes slightly warmer---not dramatically
brighter.

**NARRATOR:**\
"For the first time in a long while, Lumi's little flame flickered
happily."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 01:20--01:36 --- Into the Forest

**VISUAL:** Golden late-afternoon forest. Maya walks along a trail
carrying Lumi and gathering wildflowers.

Wide shot → medium walking shot → Lumi gently swinging in Maya's hand.

Far behind the mountains, dark clouds form.

**NARRATOR:**\
"That evening, Maya carried Lumi along a forest path while collecting
wildflowers."

**AUDIO:** Birds, leaves, gentle music.

The first distant rumble of thunder interrupts the peaceful soundscape.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 01:36--01:57 --- The Storm

**VISUAL:** Clouds roll rapidly across the sky. Trees bend. Rain begins.

Maya turns around.

Branches fall across the trail. Wind sends leaves swirling everywhere.

**NARRATOR:**\
"But beyond the mountains, a storm was gathering."

"The wind began to howl. Rain crashed through the trees, and the
familiar path disappeared."

**AUDIO:** Music becomes tense. Wind, thunder, rain and branches
cracking.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 01:57--02:10 --- Bellwood Disappears

**VISUAL:** From Maya's perspective, Bellwood is faintly visible across
the valley.

Lightning.

Then---

The Great Lantern goes out.

Village lights disappear behind sheets of rain.

**SFX:** Powerful **WHOOSH**, followed briefly by near-silence.

**NARRATOR:**\
"Then the Great Lantern went dark."

**VISUAL:** Close-up of Maya.

**NARRATOR:**\
"Maya could no longer see Bellwood."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 02:10--02:29 --- Lumi Almost Goes Out

**VISUAL:** Extreme close-up of Lumi.

Wind enters through her vents. Her flame bends violently.

Left.

Right.

Almost gone.

**LUMI --- INTERNAL VOICE:**\
"I'm too small."

**VISUAL:** Maya cups both hands around Lumi.

**MAYA, whispering:**\
"Please don't go out."

**AUDIO:** Storm becomes muffled. Music almost disappears.

Hold briefly on Lumi.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 02:29--02:48 --- The Turning Point

**NARRATOR:**\
"Lumi couldn't light the whole forest."

**VISUAL:** Darkness surrounding Maya.

**NARRATOR:**\
"She couldn't light the whole mountain."

Pause.

**NARRATOR:**\
"But perhaps... she didn't need to."

**VISUAL:** Lumi steadies her flame.

A small warm circle of light spreads across the ground.

**PRODUCTION CONSTRAINT:** Do **not** make Lumi suddenly powerful or
dramatically brighter.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 02:48--03:05 --- The Next Step

**VISUAL:** Within Lumi's small pool of light, Maya discovers an old
stone marker bearing an arrow.

She follows it.

Another small pool of light reveals another marker.

Then another.

**NARRATOR:**\
"One tiny pool of light at a time, Lumi showed Maya the next step."

**AUDIO:** Hopeful musical motif begins quietly beneath the storm.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 03:05--03:23 --- Bellwood Sees Lumi

**VISUAL:** Villagers gather beneath the dark Great Lantern tower.

They stare toward the black forest.

An elderly woman notices something.

**OLD WOMAN:**\
"Look!"

**VISUAL:** Far between the trees---

Tiny golden flicker.

Gone.

Then visible again.

**VILLAGER:**\
"There!"

**AUDIO:** Hopeful motif grows.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 03:23--03:39 --- A Path of Lights

**VISUAL:** One villager raises a lantern.

Another joins.

Then ten.

Then dozens.

Wide shot: hundreds of villagers line Bellwood's edge, their individual
lanterns combining into a glowing path.

**NARRATOR:**\
"One light became ten."

"Ten became fifty."

"And soon hundreds of little lights were shining together."

**AUDIO:** Major emotional musical build.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 03:39--03:53 --- Home

**VISUAL:** Maya sees the golden path through the trees.

**MAYA:**\
"Home!"

She runs toward Bellwood.

Villagers rush forward as she emerges from the forest.

Maya is soaked and muddy.

She looks down at Lumi.

Lumi is scratched and battered.

Her tiny flame is still burning.

**AUDIO:** Storm begins fading. Emotional musical resolution.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 03:53--04:12 --- Lumi Understands

**VISUAL:** Later. Clouds separate.

The Great Lantern burns again above Bellwood.

Lumi looks upward at it.

Then she looks around.

Hundreds of ordinary lanterns glow throughout the village.

**NARRATOR:**\
"For the first time, Lumi did not wish that she were brighter."

"Because now she understood."

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 04:12--04:29 --- Final Message

**VISUAL:** Maya carries Lumi along a quiet village street. Their warm
light moves gently over the stones.

Slowly pull upward to reveal Bellwood glowing beneath the mountains.

**NARRATOR:**\
"A light doesn't have to illuminate the whole world."

Pause.

"Sometimes..."

"It only needs to illuminate the next step."

**AUDIO:** Music reaches its emotional resolution.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 04:29--04:35 --- End

**VISUAL:** Fade to dark.

Text appears:

**THE LITTLE LANTERN**

Then:

*Even the smallest light can make a difference.*

**AUDIO:** Music resolves and fades.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Production Notes

## Runtime Target

Approximate target runtime: **4 minutes 35 seconds**.

Timing remains provisional until voiceover tests and the first rough
edit. Individual shot lengths will be refined during the shot-list and
editing stages.

## Visual Continuity Rule

Lumi must remain visually recognisable throughout the film.

Her emotional growth must **not** be represented by transforming her
into a larger, newer or dramatically more powerful lantern.

Her small light is the point of the story.

## Climax Visual Motif

Preserve the approved visual progression:

**Darkness → tiny circle of light → another circle → distant light →
many lights → path of light**

## AI Video Generation Strategy

Long script sections will **not** be treated as single AI-video
generations.

Each section will later be divided into short controlled shots.

Example:

``` text
01:20–01:36 — Into the Forest

Shot A — Maya enters forest       ~5 sec
Shot B — Maya gathers flowers     ~4 sec
Shot C — Lumi swinging in hand    ~3 sec
Shot D — storm clouds forming     ~4 sec
```

Exact shot durations will be determined during the Scene & Shot List
stage.

# Workflow Improvement 001

## Original Order

``` text
Script
↓
Character Bible
↓
Storyboard
↓
Scene List
```

## Revised Production Order

``` text
Script
↓
Character Bible
↓
Visual Style Bible
↓
Scene & Shot List
↓
Storyboard
```

## Reason

For controlled AI filmmaking, the production should determine which
shots are required **before** generating storyboard frames.

The Character Bible and Visual Style Bible must also be established
first so storyboard images preserve character and visual continuity.

This change becomes part of the AI Video Studio production standard
beginning with Video 01.
